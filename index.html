About me

I am an AI and data science engineering student at the National Higher School of Artificial Intelligence (ENSIA) in Algeria, expecting to graduate in June 2027. Over the past two years I have trained and evaluated machine learning and deep learning models across computer vision, natural language processing and time-series problems, working mainly in PyTorch, TensorFlow and Scikit-learn.

My experience spans a computer vision internship on object detection and OCR, a forecasting project for a national telecom operator, and, most recently, building a private, on-device AI assistant that keeps sensitive company data off external servers. That last project pulled me toward a broader interest: large language models and retrieval-augmented generation, and the harder problem of making AI systems people can actually trust — explainable, privacy-preserving, and careful enough to support decisions in high-stakes domains like finance and security.

I am currently looking for a PFE research internship in a group where methods are rigorous, ideas get challenged, and the work leaves a measurable trace.

Research interests

  • Low-resource and dialectal NLP
  • AI privacy and trustworthy AI
  • Explainable and interpretable AI
  • AI-assisted decision-making
